Editorial still-life photograph of a Brazilian instalment payment booklet lying closed on a dark walnut wooden table. The booklet is shaped like a chequebook: a long, narrow, landscape stack of about twelve thin cream paper slips, glued and stapled along its short left edge. Each slip has a narrow stub on the left, about one fifth of its width, separated from the main part by a faint vertical dotted line of perforations; the rest of the slip is perfectly smooth and flat, with no fold or crease. Only the top slip is visible, its right corner lifting slightly. The top slip is a simple form printed in black ink on cream paper: at the top left, the heading "MAIO 2027" in a small elegant serif typeface; at the top right, the small number "05/12"; beneath them, a few thin horizontal ruled lines; at the bottom right, the word "SOBRA" in small capitals next to an empty ruled box. A red rubber-stamp impression reading "PAGO", slightly tilted and unevenly inked, sits on the right side of the slip. Nothing else is written or printed on the slip. A plain wooden pencil lies on the table next to the booklet. Soft natural window light from the left in the late afternoon, long gentle shadows, visible paper fibre and wood grain. Shot on a 50mm lens at f/2.8 from a three-quarter top-down angle, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, natural muted colours, calm and dignified mood. Generous empty table surface to the right of the booklet.
